# Series Intro · Dad's English Barbershop

English is the only spoken language. English captions appear above Chinese translations.

| Beat | Daughter's English narration | 中文字幕 | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | My dad has a barbershop here in America. After school, I come right here. | 我爸爸在美国开了一家理发店。放学后，我就来这里。 | Father and daughter outside the shop. |
| 2 | Dad has a big barber chair. And I have a little toy corner. | 爸爸有一把大大的理发椅。我有一个小小的玩具角。 | Father, chair, daughter's toy car. |
| 3 | Emma is always in a hurry. Mr. Miller loves talking about his dog, Milo. | 艾玛总是有点赶时间。米勒爷爷爱讲他的小狗米洛。 | Two returning customers inside the shop. |
| 4 | Our neighbor runs the coffee shop. Sometimes she brings us coffee. | 邻居阿姨开着隔壁的咖啡店。有时候她会给我们带咖啡。 | Neighbor carrying coffee. |
| 5 | When someone opens the door, Dad smiles and says: “Come on in!” I say it, too! | 有人推门进来，爸爸就笑着说：“进来吧！”我也跟着说！ | Father and daughter by the door; expression card. |
| 6 | I learn a new English phrase here every day. Come back tomorrow for another little story from our shop. | 我每天都在这里学一句新英语。明天再来听理发店里的小故事吧。 | Daughter shows father her drawing. |
| 7 | See you tomorrow! | 明天见！ | Bilingual end card. |

“Come on in!” is the first useful phrase. The daily episodes that follow use English conversations with bilingual subtitles.
